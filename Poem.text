Lines of Code

In the quiet hum of a humming machine,
a cursor blinks where nothing has been.
A thought takes shape, a function named,
small and true, and slowly framed.

Each commit a step, each branch a thread,
weaving the work that lies ahead.
No grand design, no rushing haste—
just careful hands, and time not wasted.

And when the tests all pass in green,
we glimpse the shape of what we mean:
a repository, line by line,
a story built, in its own time.
