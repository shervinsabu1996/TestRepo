Fresh Clone

A morning pull, a clean checkout,
the working tree with nothing out.
No merge to fight, no stash to keep—
just open ground where ideas sleep.

We plant a branch, we name it well,
and watch it grow, and watch it swell
with edits small and edits bold,
a diff that tells what wasn't told.

Then comes the push, the quiet request,
a pull request waiting for its test.
And somewhere far, a reviewer's eye
will read the change, and wonder why—

then smile, approve, and merge it in,
another story lets begin.
